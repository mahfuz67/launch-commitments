# Product walkthrough — target 2 minutes 30 seconds

Use the actual hosted devnet app. Label any older local recording as local; do not splice it into a claimed public execution. Cut waiting periods with a visible “after the funding deadline” caption.

0:00–0:15 — Open the app: “A community wants to launch together. Their contributions should execute the agreed launch—or be recoverable without the organizer.”

0:15–0:35 — Show target, minimum output, deadline and fee recipient. “These are the terms contributors see before signing. Setup costs are paid separately by the organizer.”

0:35–0:55 — Show a contribution and withdrawal/receipt. “Contributors keep their own wallet keys and can withdraw before the window closes.”

0:55–1:20 — Show the successful public campaign and its launch receipt. “A contributor executed token creation, the DBC pool and the first purchase in one transaction. The organizer did not sign. Each contributor then claimed a proportional allocation and excess SOL.”

1:20–1:40 — Show graduation, the DAMM pool and a sale receipt only after public verification. “The token continues through Meteora's lifecycle into DAMM v2.”

1:40–2:05 — Show the underfunded and expired campaigns, refund totals and standalone recovery receipts. “No launch means recovery under the published rules. These refunds executed with the app server switched off.”

2:05–2:30 — Show source and limitations. “This is an open-source devnet prototype with developer-run evidence. It is upgradeable and unaudited; refunds do not cover trading losses. Try the workflow and inspect every transaction.”
